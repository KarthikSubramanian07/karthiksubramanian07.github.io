# Privacy

This is Karthik Subramanian’s personal portfolio, hosted on GitHub Pages. The site has no account registration, payment flow, contact form, or site-installed analytics. Its own code does not set cookies or use local storage to track visitors.

## Hosting and fonts

Loading a page requires your browser to request files from the hosting service. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of request information is governed by its own privacy statement. The site also loads typography from Google Fonts, so your browser may make requests to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Those services receive information needed to serve the requested font files, including your network address.

[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

[Google Fonts privacy information](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy)

## Browser interactions

The visual effects and interactive controls on the homepage and developer profile run in your browser. The developer profile loads a public statistics file from this site. When you choose an email control, your browser can open your mail application; when you choose a copy control, it can write the selected text to your clipboard. This site does not receive the resulting email message or send clipboard contents to a server.

## External links and contact

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other external destinations take you to services with their own privacy practices. An email or message sent through a published contact channel is handled by that channel’s provider rather than a form on this website. Use the contact page to find the published channels, and share only the information needed for your conversation.

[Contact information](https://karthiksubramanian07.github.io/contact/)
